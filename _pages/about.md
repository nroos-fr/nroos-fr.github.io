I am a master student in applied maths and machine learning at the [master MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) ("Mathématiques, Vision, Apprentissage")
at the [ENS Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/en/school/ens-paris-saclay),
as part of a double degree program with my home engineering school [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/school/open/core-mission)
(part of [Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/about/about-us)).

I have already two major research experiences:
- One-year long research track supervised by [Enzo Tartaglione](https://enzotarta.github.io/) on bias amplification by diffusion models during my first year of master studies, which
lead to a [first-author publication](/_publications/2026-03-04-how-i-met-your-bias.md) in the Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision 2026 (main track).
- A six-month research internship at KU Leuven during my gap year, where I worked with Dmytro Rizdvanetskyi (then PhD student) and [Pavlo Lutsik](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PB9nc_0AAAAJ&hl=en) on cell type deconvolution from DNA methylation data. We developed a data-driven soft labeling scheme and state-of-the-art deep learning framework for read-level cell type deconvolution scaling to 39 cell types. Our paper was accepted at NeurIPS 2026 (main track).

I am currently **interested in fundamental or applicative research in machine learning / applied mathematics in fields with potential applications in biology, healthcare, chemistry and physics**.
I am looking for a **6-month end-of-study research internship in these areas starting in April 2027**, preferably as a first step before a PhD program.
